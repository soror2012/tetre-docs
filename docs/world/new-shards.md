# New Shards

A **Shard** is a crystal infused with stabilized elemental energy. **New Shards** are the ones in circulation today, made in quantity by the Crystal Sages' successors after the Otalla catastrophe.

## What they do

- **Revitalize Ward Nodes.** Bring a New Shard to a dimming Node and it brightens.
- **Are carried by guardians.** The Elites keep a supply for emergencies.

They do not make their bearers special. Shards are infrastructure, like fuel or spare parts, and the game never treats a Shard as a chosen hero's birthright.

## What they are not

Shards are **not elemental**. There is no Fire Shard or Ice Shard. Elements are a combat matter (see [Combat](/systems/combat)); Shards belong to the Ward.
