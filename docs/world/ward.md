# The Ward

The Ward is the world's overarching elemental stabilization system. Centuries ago, the **Crystal Sages** created it, because raw elemental energy was too volatile to support large-scale civilization.

## Radiance and Hollow

Elemental energy exists in two broad states, and the public understands them like this:

| | Radiance | Hollow |
|---|---|---|
| **State** | Stable | Unstable |
| **Character** | Harmonizes with nature | Erratic and hard to regulate |
| **Effects** | Clean water, fertile land, breathable air, working magic | Poisoned ground, twisted wildlife, spreading corruption |
| **Role** | The foundation of civilization | A hazard to be kept away from it |

Neither is a combat element. They are the two moods of the world's energy, and they belong to the setting, not to the battle system.

## What the Ward does

According to everything taught in schools, the Ward:

- steadies raw elemental energy,
- encourages the formation of Radiance,
- distributes stabilized energy across the world, and
- prevents catastrophic regional collapse.

It is the reason the Academy gardens bloom. A groundskeeper will tell you the soil is "Radiance-rich", and that tulips grow the size of your head if you leave them alone.

## Who built it

The Crystal Sages are remembered as founders of stability. Their work is in every Ward Node, and their successors still make the Shards that keep the Nodes bright. The Ward is treated with something between engineering confidence and religious respect.
