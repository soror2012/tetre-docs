# Ward Nodes

A **Ward Node** is a crystalline formation that grows out of the ground at an important place. Nodes are the physical presence of the Ward: where the network anchors to the land.

**They are not machines.** There are no gears or consoles. A Node is a tall crystal formation with facets that shift from sea-green to pale glass, a slow gold pulse deep inside, and faint motes drifting upward.

## What people do with them

- **Revitalize them.** When a Node dims, people bring [New Shards](./new-shards) to it.
- **Guard them.** Each nation maintains or protects its own Nodes. In Grand Cross, the Elites do.
- **Visit them.** Nodes are important places for regional exploration and for story moments.

At the Academy, a notice on the courtyard board reads:

> **WARD CHAMBERS:** Closed to the public during Elite maintenance.
