# Modern-Fantasy Civilization

Tetre is not a medieval world. It is a fantasy world that kept growing.

| You will see | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Everyday infrastructure** | Paved streets, iron streetlamps, trams, bridges |
| **Transport** | Cars, cargo balloons, airships. The first airship of the day leaves Grand Cross for Halford at dawn |
| **Industry and engineering** | Halford's engines, docks and trade lines |
| **Institutions** | Courts, academies, military installations |
| **Elemental magic** | Part of daily life, from gardens to engineering |
| **Ruins and ancient magic** | Crystal structures and old places that predate every nation |

Students gossip about a new tram line, trade tea by cargo balloon and argue about who has the best airship docks. Grand Cross is hospitable and busy. The look is **warm cream stone, violet banners and gold trim**, with dark-navy ironwork against pale buildings (see [Art Direction](/art/)).

## Design intent

- A world that feels closer to modern society, not a medieval pastiche.
- Magic and engineering coexist and neither is the villain.
- Technology never becomes the story's dystopia. It is simply how people live.
