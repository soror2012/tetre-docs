# Hollow Zones

A **Hollow Zone** is a region where Hollow has overtaken the natural environment. The term replaced an earlier working name, "Deadzone", because the land is not dead. It is unstable.

## What you find there

- Desaturated, gray-washed landscapes
- Distorted wildlife and monstrous creatures
- Abandoned settlements
- Corrupted vegetation
- Unstable elemental phenomena
- Dangerous remnants of civilization

Hollow itself reads as **wrongness and instability**, never as a demon. Visually it follows a flat, desaturated gray ramp. It is not sentient and not evil.

## Otalla

**Otalla** is the most significant known Hollow Zone, the site of a catastrophe within living memory. Survivors rebuilt as [New Otalla](/nations/new-otalla).

The Academy remembers Otalla with a plaque by the courtyard fountain:

> *"In honor of Rowan and the Keepers, who gave their lives containing the Otalla breach."*

Someone leaves fresh flowers there every year.
