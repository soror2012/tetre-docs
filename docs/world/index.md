# Tetre

Tetre is a world of several nations, each with its own government, culture, military and way of living with elemental energy. Its people have built cities, rail lines and airships on top of an ancient promise: that the elements will stay steady.

Most people trust that promise without understanding how it works. The textbooks at Grand Cross Academy give it one line on a chalkboard:

> **ELEMENTAL STATES**
> Radiance: stable. Hollow: unstable.
> The Ward guides energy toward Radiance.
> *(Exam Friday. Someone has drawn a small sun in the corner.)*

## Tone

Tetre is hopeful, colorful and lived in. Some principles shape every page of the lore:

- **Not dystopian.** The Ward genuinely saved civilization, and cities are pleasant places to live.
- **Hollow is not evil.** It is a condition of instability, not a creature with a will.
- **No cartoon villains.** Leaders on every side want to protect their people and disagree about how.
- **No chosen one.** People matter because of what they decide.
