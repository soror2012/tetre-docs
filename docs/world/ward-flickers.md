# Ward Flickers

A **Ward Flicker** is a rare, visible disturbance in the Ward's stability. For a moment the sky or ceiling shows a pale crystalline lattice, the color drains out of the world, and the ground shakes slightly.

## What people say

The public explanation is simple: the Ward has met **a significant elemental fluctuation and is working to stabilize it.** People trust that it will.

Flickers are rare enough that everyone recognizes their significance. They are not everyday weather. A student in the courtyard puts it this way:

> "Ward Flickers are rare fluctuations. The Ward stabilizes them. My grandmother saw one, once. She still will not talk about it."

## How they look and sound

| Level | Duration | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | A brief flash, about a third of a second | Colors desaturate toward gray, a lattice flickers across the sky, the camera shudders and the audio dips |
| **2** | Longer, with several pulses | The same, but stronger, and a nearby Node stays visibly strained for the rest of the scene |

In the early game the player sees Flickers of increasing intensity. Where they lead is for the player to find out.
