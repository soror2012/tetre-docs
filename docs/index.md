## What is Tetre?

Tetre is a narrative-driven 2D JRPG in the tradition of *Final Fantasy III* and *VI*: a colorful, explorable world, an ensemble cast, turn-based battles and a story that grows from a small corner of the map into the whole continent. It is also a modern fantasy. There are streetlamps, trams, cargo balloons and airships, and elemental magic is part of everyday life.

For centuries the world has been held steady by **the Ward**, a planet-scale crystalline network made by the Crystal Sages. It favors stable **Radiance** and keeps unstable **Hollow** in check. Gardens bloom, water runs clean, and most people never have to think about why.

You begin at **Grand Cross Academy**, among the top prospects in their year, on the morning the rankings go up and the nation's elite military is recruiting.

<div class="question">Can a world learn to live without the very system that has protected it for centuries?</div>

Silas is not the chosen one. He matters because of the choices he makes, the people he stands beside and the kind of leader he decides to be.
