# The Cast's Hidden Arcs

## Hugh: from rival to antagonist

Hugh is ambitious, impulsive and eager to join the Elites, and he accepts an offer at graduation. That separates the original group.

In the Origin Shard conflict he becomes an **antagonist**, fighting beside Wing and the Elites. **His conflict stays personal and he is not irredeemably evil.** He is Silas's true rival from the first page of the story, and the rivalry is what carries into this turn.

In combat his presence is the **Rivals' Accord** Dual Tech with Silas, which leaves the party when he joins the Elites.

## Skyler: from bodyguard to deserter

Skyler is the youngest Elite and Wing's bodyguard. During Act 1 she begins **questioning Wing's ideals**. She eventually joins Silas's group and becomes a **deserter and traitor** in the Elites' eyes. She is the bridge between Grand Cross's military structure and the developing New Keepers.

## The New Keepers

The party grows into a small independent organization, the **New Keepers**, with an FFVI-style ensemble that recruits across Tetre. Not everyone will be a warrior: engineers, mages, former soldiers, explorers, healers, scholars and survivors.

## Walter and Relna

| Leader | Position in the hidden story |
|---|---|
| **Walter** (Halford) | Wants to weaponize the Origin Shards and use Hollow against the growing threat. A major antagonist who genuinely wants to improve his nation and increasingly seeks power |
| **Lady Relna** (Narae) | Wants to understand how Origin Shards, New Shards, Radiance and Hollow relate. Believes the Origin Shards could cure or stabilize Hollow |

## Competing goals

Silas wants the Origin Shards destroyed so Rowan can't use them. His position, Wing's, Relna's and Walter's form the Act 3 argument (see [Five-Act Structure](./five-acts)).
