# The Five-Act Structure

## Act 1: The Awakening

**Where:** Grand Cross.

Several years after Otalla, Silas, Lorena, Vulcan and Hugh are 18 or 19 and among the Academy's top twenty. Hugh wants the Elites, Lorena and Vulcan are reluctant and Silas is unsure. Their Professor, a confidant of Wing, recommends them to the General, who shows them a Ward Node and talks of reviving the Keepers.

- **First Ward Flicker:** minor. Citizens explain the Ward occasionally fluctuates. It establishes the concept without revealing the truth.
- **Second Ward Flicker:** much stronger. The Elites decide a Node needs revitalizing and bring New Shards. The emergency revitalization triggers a stronger response that reaches a Ward Node in Halford.
- **The Grand Cross Incident:** Halford reads it as a serious disturbance and sends a Vultures response force. They arrive 15 to 30 minutes after the Ward event, and their arrival is mistaken for an invasion. The Elites engage, and the confused Vultures ask why Grand Cross is attacking them. Skyler gives support while the four prospects join the fighting. The Vultures retreat, and their commander accuses Wing of **faking a Ward emergency** to launch a surprise attack on Halford. The misunderstanding creates lasting political tension.
- **Ending:** Wing offers the four positions in the Elites and considers them candidates for the New Keepers. The group starts to sense something larger is happening.

## Act 2: The Journey

Wing appoints Silas, Lorena, Vulcan and Skyler as the **new Keepers**. Their mission is to inspect Ward Nodes, investigate disturbances, recruit capable people and help prevent further decline. Hugh stays with the Elites.

They discover minor Hollow incidents that have gone unnoticed (monsters in populated areas, small corruptions, affected people, unstable conditions), recruit new members and grow independent.

**Climax:** in a Hollow Zone, a **Wanderer** reveals himself as one of Rowan's original Keepers and exposes the first major truths (see [Rowan and the Keepers](./rowan-and-keepers)). The world has been built on a lie.

## Act 3: The Truth

The party investigates the history of Otalla: witnesses, records, abandoned places and members of the Council. They learn about the seven Origin Shards, Rowan's experiments, the exile, the creation of New Shards, the Council's cover-up and Wing's link to Rowan.

**Competing ideologies**

| Who | Wants |
|---|---|
| **Silas** | The Origin Shards destroyed, to keep them from Rowan |
| **Wing** | The Origin Shards recovered, to help his brother |
| **Relna** | The Origin Shards used to cure or stabilize Hollow |
| **Walter** | The Origin Shards weaponized, using Hollow against the threat |

Wing hardens once Rowan's exile becomes personal. The Council's younger members defect, and **the Council disbands**. Wing and Walter become significant antagonists.

## Act 4: The Origin Shards

The nations race to recover the seven Origin Shards, hidden in labyrinths, ruins and sealed temples. The shards are **not** power-ups, and each recovery is a unique story-and-gameplay set piece. Possible encounters include:

- Walter and the Vultures competing for an artifact
- Wing, Hugh and the Elites guarding one
- A Wanderer protecting an ancient location
- A Wanderer summoning something from a Hollow Zone
- Environmental dungeon challenges and ancient guardians

The planned split is New Keepers 2, Narae 1, Elites 2, Halford 2 (see [Shards](./shards#distribution-planned-act-4)). The New Keepers do not secure all seven, and the conflict escalates toward open war.

## Act 5: The End of the Ward

Rowan and the Wanderers grow stronger. The accumulated Hollow threatens to overwhelm the Ward, and **the Ward collapses.** The New Keepers and Tetre's surviving forces face Rowan's amalgamation of Hollow. For the finale see [Final Revelation and Epilogue](./ending).
