# The Council, Two Generations

To the public, the Council is a neutral court, school and mediator at the center of Tetre, with no say over Shard distribution.

## The elder members

Some of the older members **know the truth** about Rowan, the Origin Shards and the rewritten history. They **participated in or enabled the cover-up.**

## The new generation

Younger members from different nations come to the Council because they don't necessarily share their home nation's ideals. They seek education, mentorship and common ground. When they learn the truth, many **reject the older generation's actions**.

## The collapse

Their defection eventually causes the Council to **disband** (Act 3). The neutral institution that the nations used to settle disputes is gone just as the nations begin preparing for war.

## Why it matters

- It removes the last neutral referee between the nations.
- It lets the story say something about institutions that protect themselves.
