# Spoiler Glossary

Terms that only exist in the hidden story. For ordinary terms see the [main Glossary](/glossary).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Displacement** | What the Ward really does to Hollow: it redirects it deeper into the elemental flow instead of destroying it |
| **Origin Shards** | The seven original, powerful Shards, able to contain Hollow. Not elemental |
| **New Shards (true story)** | Weaker, reproducible replacements made after the exile. They revitalize Nodes but cannot contain Hollow |
| **The exile** | The collective decision of the nations and the Council to banish Rowan and his Keepers and seal the Origin Shards |
| **The cover-up** | The rewriting of history into the heroic account on the Academy plaque |
| **The Original Keepers** | Rowan's team, who created and maintained the Ward |
| **The Wanderers** | What the exiled Keepers became: people of the Hollow Zones who know the truth |
| **The New Keepers** | The independent organization that grows from Silas's party |
| **The Final Revelation** | Nature can stabilize itself without the Ward |
| **The Epilogue** | A Wardless world adapting |
