# Wing's Secret

**Wing is Rowan's younger brother.**

## What he knows

Wing knows the truth about Rowan's exile and the cover-up. To the world he looks like a responsible leader trying to preserve the peace, who founded the Elites to protect Grand Cross and the Ward.

## The turn

His personal grief eventually overwhelms his judgment. When the truth becomes public, **Wing turns against Silas and his companions.** He wants to recover the Origin Shards to help his brother. He becomes a major Act 3 and 4 antagonist.

**He is not the final boss.** The story culminates in a confrontation in which Silas and his companions may have to **kill him**.

His motive is personal grief, not a cartoon-villain agenda.

## Why he matters now

- The Elites, his creation, are the Grand Cross military that holds Origin Shards in Act 4.
- His confidant, the Professor, is part of the early Act 1 setup.
- Skyler, his bodyguard, is the first person close to him to question his ideals (see [The Cast's Hidden Arcs](./cast-arcs)).
