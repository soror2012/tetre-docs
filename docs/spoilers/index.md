## Ground rules for the hidden story

These constraints hold for everything in this section.

- **No chosen one.** Silas matters through decisions, relationships and leadership, not destiny.
- **Shards are not elemental power-ups.** There is no Fire Shard or Ice Shard.
- **Hollow is not evil or sentient.** It is instability.
- **No cartoon villains.** Every antagonist has reasons that are understandable, and several begin as people the player likes.
- **Not a dystopia.** The Ward genuinely saved civilization.
- **No excessive exposition.** Reveal through places, people and choices.

::: warning Act 1 discipline
In Act 1 the game must **never hint** at the hidden truth explicitly. The public understanding of the Ward is presented as the player's honest understanding of the world.
:::
