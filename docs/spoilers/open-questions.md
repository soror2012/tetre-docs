# Open Design Questions

These are **deliberately undecided**. Nothing on this site invents an answer to them.

## Lore and story

1. The names and locations of all seven Origin Shards.
2. The personalities and histories of the original Keepers.
3. The precise mechanics of how Origin Shards contain Hollow.
4. The complete history of the Otalla catastrophe.
5. The precise circumstances of Wing's death.
6. The exact nature of the final Rowan confrontation.
7. The full visual representation of the Ward around the planet.

## World

8. The geographical layout of Tetre.
9. The names of the remaining nations, including the nature-aligned nation.
10. Whether the game uses a world map, linked regional maps or a hybrid.

## Cast and systems

11. The complete roster of New Keepers and the final party size.
12. Exact combat progression and equipment beyond the demo.
13. Names for the Professor, the Vultures' commander and Academy students (placeholders allowed).

## Presentation

14. The approach to music, sound design and cinematics. The demo is silent so far.
