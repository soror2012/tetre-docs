# Rowan and the Original Keepers

## The public version

> *"In honor of Rowan and the Keepers, who gave their lives containing the Otalla breach."*

Rowan and his Keepers are remembered as the greatest heroes in Tetre's history, who died saving the world at Otalla. The Academy keeps a plaque and someone leaves flowers every year.

## What happened

Rowan was intelligent, compassionate and committed to protecting civilization. He helped establish and maintain the Ward with his team of Keepers. After learning that the Ward was only displacing Hollow (see [The Hidden Truth](./hidden-truth)), he looked for a fix, and found that the [Origin Shards](./shards) could contain Hollow.

Instead of being celebrated, he was condemned. The nations and the Council feared sacred artifacts holding Hollow. They collectively decided to:

- **exile** Rowan and his Keepers,
- **confiscate** the Origin Shards,
- **seal** them in various places, and
- **erase the truth** from recorded history.

Rowan kept a few Origin Shards. The official account was rewritten into the heroic death on the plaque.

## Rowan's transformation

In exile, Rowan and the surviving Keepers adapted. They learned to use the Origin Shards to interact with and contain Hollow, and over time became **entwined with the energy they had tried to control**. They eventually became the **source of the Hollow threat** now endangering Tetre.

Their motives are betrayal, abandonment and revenge. Rowan thinks the world that condemned him deserves to feel the consequences of its choices.

**He is not simply evil.** His intentions were noble. His tragedy is that his pursuit of justice became indistinguishable from vengeance. In Act 5 the player faces his grotesque amalgamation of Hollow energy.

## The Original Keepers become the Wanderers

After the exile the original Keepers became **the Wanderers**. They live in the Hollow Zones and have spent years exploring the infected wastelands. They know the truth about the Ward and the Origin Shards.

They gradually become antagonists and sources of information. One of them is the **Act 2 revelation character**.

## What the Act 2 Wanderer reveals

At the climax of Act 2 the party meets a Wanderer in a Hollow Zone, revealed to be one of Rowan's original Keepers. He exposes the first major truths:

- Rowan and the Keepers did not die at Otalla.
- The history was fabricated.
- The Origin Shards exist.
- The Ward does not destroy Hollow.
- Rowan and his followers are alive.

The party realizes the world was built on a lie.
