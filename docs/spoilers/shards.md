# Origin Shards and New Shards

## The Origin Shards

There are exactly **seven**, created when the Ward was established. They are considerably more powerful and efficient than the Shards in circulation.

- **They are not elemental.** No Fire Shard, no Ice Shard. Each recovery is a distinct narrative and gameplay experience, not a power-up.
- They were used by Rowan and the first Keepers to revitalize the Ward.
- After Otalla, Rowan discovered they could **contain displaced Hollow** and passively destabilize its concentrated formations, which means they could address the Ward's underlying flaw.
- That discovery is what condemned him (see [Rowan](./rowan-and-keepers)).
- Hollow in them leaves a trace Rowan can sense. Collecting, moving or destroying them influences his awareness of their location.

The existence of the seven is an **ancient political secret**. Only select officials know.

## The New Shards

After the exile, the nations had the remaining Crystal Sages produce replacements.

| | Origin Shards | New Shards |
|---|---|---|
| Number | Seven, ever | Many, reproducible |
| Power | Concentrated, efficient | Weaker, spread thin |
| Revitalize Ward Nodes | Yes | Yes |
| Contain or destabilize Hollow | **Yes** | No |
| Publicly known | **No** | Yes, believed to be the only Shards that survived |

New Shards can maintain the Ward for a while. They cannot solve its problem.

## Locations

The Origin Shards were confiscated and sealed in ancient labyrinths, abandoned ruins, sealed temples and other dangerous places. **The exact locations are undecided** (see [Open Design Questions](./open-questions)).
