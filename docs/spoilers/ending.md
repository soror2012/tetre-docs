# Final Revelation and Epilogue

## The final battle

The seven Origin Shards are brought together. Rowan unleashes an enormous surge of Hollow. The Origin Shards **absorb the attack**, begin to crack and eventually **all seven shatter**. The stored Hollow is neutralized through the final sacrifice of the artifacts. The Ward begins to fracture and dissipate into the atmosphere.

## The Final Revelation

The party panics. They believe the Ward's disappearance will make all elemental energy unstable. **Nothing catastrophic happens.** Nature starts to stabilize itself.

The Ward was never the source of life. It was a system meant to help nature recover, and civilization became dependent on it. This answers the game's central question:

> Can a world learn to live without the very system that has protected it for centuries?

It can.

::: warning Act 1 discipline
The final revelation must **never be hinted at explicitly in Act 1**.
:::

## The Epilogue

- The Ward no longer exists, and the Origin Shards have shattered.
- There is no more need for Shards or Ward Nodes.
- The world has to learn to exist without the infrastructure that sustained it.
- Nature is not suddenly perfect. Weather becomes less predictable, magic changes and civilization has to adapt.
- **Tetre survives.**
- The New Keepers' original purpose becomes unnecessary. The world no longer needs a group of heroes to maintain an artificial balance.
