# Glossary

Spoiler-safe definitions, as a citizen of Grand Cross would use them. Terms that only matter to the hidden story are in the [⚠ Spoiler Glossary](/spoilers/glossary).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Ward** | The planet-wide crystalline stabilization network built by the Crystal Sages to steady the world's elemental forces. See [The Ward](/world/ward). |
| **Ward Node** | A crystalline formation that anchors the Ward at a given place. Not a machine. Each nation maintains or guards its own. See [Ward Nodes](/world/ward-nodes). |
| **Ward Flicker** | A rare, visible disturbance in the Ward's stability. Publicly explained as a significant elemental fluctuation that the Ward is stabilizing. See [Ward Flickers](/world/ward-flickers). |
| **Radiance** | The stable state of elemental energy. It supports clean water, fertile land, breathable air and working magic. A lore concept, **not** a combat element. |
| **Hollow** | The unstable state of elemental energy: erratic, hard to regulate, able to poison land and twist wildlife. A condition, not a creature, and **not** a combat element. |
| **Hollow Zone** | A region where Hollow has overtaken the natural environment. Otalla is the best known. Replaces the early working term "Deadzone". |
| **Shard** | A crystal used to revitalize Ward Nodes. See [New Shards](/world/new-shards). |
| **New Shards** | The Shards in circulation today. |
| **Crystal Sages** | The founders of the Ward and the makers of Shards. |
| **Otalla** | The site of the great catastrophe and the most significant Hollow Zone. Its survivors founded New Otalla. |
| **The Elites** | Grand Cross's elite military division, founded by General Wing. |
| **The Vultures** | Halford's tactical soldiers: disciplined, engineered gear, matter-of-fact. |
| **The Templars** | Narae's soldiers, specialists in defensive magic. |
| **The Council** | The neutral court, school and mediator at the center of Tetre. |
| **The Destined** | The prestigious special forces group General Wing once led. |
| **Keepers** | The title borne by the heroes who looked after the Ward. There is talk in Grand Cross of reviving it. |
| **Tech** | A special combat ability that costs MP. |
| **Combo Tech** | A Tech that fuses two or three party members' turns into one. See [Combo Techs](/systems/combo-techs). |
| **Trait** | A character's always-on signature combat mechanic. |
| **Passive** | A combat bonus that unlocks at a given level. |
| **Tick** | The unit of battle time on the timeline. |
| **Timeline** | The strip at the top of the battle screen showing the next eight turns. |
| **Elements** | Fire, Ice, Lightning, Earth and Wind (plus non-elemental). |
| **Prospect** | A top-ranked Academy student being watched by the Elites. |
