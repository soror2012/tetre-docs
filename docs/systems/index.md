# Game Systems

Tetre is built around three things, in this order: **exploration**, **combat** and **character progression**. Story is delivered through places, people and optional conversations rather than long cutscenes, so most of your time is spent playing.

## Design pillars

1. **Every character is a different verb.** Silas alternates, Lorena builds and cashes in, Vulcan loafs then erupts, Hugh over-protects.
2. **Read the timeline, plan the combo.** Turn order is always visible, and combos reward lining up partners' turns.
3. **Relationships are mechanics.** Combos mirror the cast's bonds.
4. **Short, punchy fights.** A standard encounter takes two to four minutes. No grinding is needed in the demo.

## Encounters are placed

There are **no random battles.** Every fight is placed in the world or triggered by a scripted event, so you always know why it is happening.

## Inspiration

Combo Techs are inspired by the multi-character techs of *Chrono Trigger*. We borrow the idea of the mechanic only. All names, effects, characters and presentation are original.
