# Lessons from the Reference

The look of exploration was guided by a single piece of reference concept art: a 3/4-view town plaza. We don't republish it here. We take its **qualities** (palette warmth, density, wear, lighting), never its specific buildings, statue or characters.

| What the reference shows | The rule for Tetre |
|---|---|
| A high 3/4 camera: facades show their front face and floors read as planes | **3/4 top-down orthogonal grid**, with walls 1 to 3 tiles tall and a lit top row |
| Warm cream sandstone, arched stained-glass windows, violet banners with gold emblems | This became **Grand Cross**: cream stone, violet and gold heraldry. The emblem is our own gold cross-diamond |
| Mauve-rose flagstone with darker grout, worn cobble paths, moss in the cracks | Ground has texture and wear, with seeded variation per tile |
| Dense flowers, ivy and planters, a willow tree, layered greens | **Radiance is abundance.** Healthy places are lush |
| Dark-navy iron lampposts and benches | Street furniture is cool and dark against warm stone |
| Teal water with light ripples | Water in a teal ramp, with shimmer planned |
| Soft dark outlines, gentle top-left light | Ink outlines, never black. Shadows fall bottom-right |
| Small readable characters with distinct hats and colors | A signature color and silhouette feature per character |

## Where we are

![The Academy courtyard in the current build](/img/w3-planters.png)

The build is close on structure, and the polish backlog is about density and warmth: the paving still reads a little gray against the reference's rose flagstone, flower beds can be more generous, and the ground is flat where the reference has terraces. Portraits currently share one face template and will diverge.
