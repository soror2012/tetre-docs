Skyler is the youngest of the Elites and General Wing's personal bodyguard. She is exceptionally gifted with ranged weapons, yet humble and considerate. She respects her skill and uses it responsibly.

She appears in the opening demo as a non-player character and takes part in its climactic encounter.
