Vulcan is Silas's childhood friend: eccentric, fun-loving and occasionally lazy. He brings humor, levity and warmth. The first time you meet him he is under the willow, napping, "having a moment" with the tree.

He and Silas built a strong partnership during their Academy years. **Silas taught Vulcan swordsmanship and Vulcan taught Silas magic.** In Vulcan's telling, he got the worse deal: "Your sword is heavy."

He relies on magic but can look after himself in a scuffle, and his reluctance about the Elites is mostly that "the uniforms look itchy."

## Relationships

- **Silas**: childhood friend and mutual teacher. In the courtyard, Vulcan offers to spar after class. Your answer is a small branching choice.
- **Lorena**: partner in the *Firebrand* combo.
