# The Cast

Four friends at Grand Cross Academy stand at the center of the opening: Silas, Lorena, Vulcan and Hugh. All four are around 18 or 19 and rank among the Academy's top twenty prospects. Around them are the people who shape their choices.

## Signature colors

Every character has a signature color and a silhouette feature, so the cast reads at 16 x 24 pixels.

| Character | Signature color | Silhouette hook |
|---|---|---|
| Silas | Violet Academy coat, cream scarf | Tousled dark-brown hair, sword at the hip |
| Lorena | Crimson jacket | High ponytail, gauntlets, a forward-leaning stance |
| Vulcan | Gold/amber trim over violet | Messy copper hair, oversized sleeves, a lazy slouch |
| Hugh | White and cream with gold trim | Neat short blond hair, upright posture |
| Wing | Deep navy greatcoat, gold epaulettes | Tall, gray-streaked hair, cape |
| Skyler | Teal Elite uniform | Short silver-blue bob, long rifle on the back |
| Professor | Muted plum robes | Spectacles, a book |

## How they relate

- **Silas and Hugh** are true rivals. They are two natural leaders who clash because Hugh acts first and thinks second.
- **Silas and Vulcan** are childhood friends who taught each other: Silas swordwork, Vulcan magic.
- **Silas and Lorena** are friendly competitors. She keeps a running score.

Their portraits here are the game's current placeholder art. Final art will follow the same palette and silhouette rules.
