Lorena is brave, competitive and determined to prove she can stand with anyone. She won't be defined by the Academy or by anyone's uniform:

> "When people say my name, I want it to be mine. Not the Academy's. Not anyone's."

She met Silas when they were young and has been keeping score ever since. **She is a friendly competitor, not his main rival.** The score is affectionate ("Eleven to ten. Just so we both remember."). She has her own motivations beyond Silas, and she is reluctant about joining the Elites.

Her determination lets her keep fighting even when a fight looks hopeless.

## Relationships

- **Silas**: friendly competition, a running tally and real loyalty.
- **Vulcan**: the Academy's other half of the trio, and the source of her *Firebrand* combo.
