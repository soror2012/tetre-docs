Silas is kind and hopeful. He believes there is usually a peaceful solution, and he is not afraid to fight when there isn't one. He is among Grand Cross Academy's top prospects and is being seriously considered for the Elites, but he is not sure he wants to become part of the General's military machine.

He is not the chosen one. He matters because of his decisions, his relationships and the kind of leader he chooses to be. His arc is about **leadership and responsibility**, and learning when peaceful ideals have to be defended through action.

## Relationships

- **Hugh**, his true rival. See [Hugh](./hugh).
- **Vulcan**, his oldest friend and sparring partner. They taught each other.
- **Lorena**, a friendly competitor.
