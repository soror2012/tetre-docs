The Professor teaches the Academy's top prospects and is a close confidant of General Wing. Dry and fond of the four, they gave Wing four names when he asked for the Academy's best:

> "General Wing has asked to meet the Academy's top prospects. I gave him four names."

They are the one who tells the group that "there is talk the General may revive the title of Keepers. Rumor, mind." Their parting advice: *"Be punctual, be honest, be yourselves."*
