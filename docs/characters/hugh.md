Hugh is ambitious, determined and, at first, a little reserved. That doesn't last: he becomes the vocal leader of the group. He eagerly wants to join the Elites, rehearses speeches and greets Silas with "That is not a coincidence. That is destiny with a seating chart."

He is **Silas's true rival.** The two clash often, because both are natural leaders and Hugh is impulsive. Hugh graduates near the top of the class alongside Silas, and the Elites' recruitment matters to him more than to anyone else in the group.

## Relationships

- **Silas**: the real rivalry. Competing leaders, always in each other's way. See the [Rivals' Accord](/systems/combo-techs#rivals-accord) combo.
- **Lorena and Vulcan**: reluctant about the Elites, unlike him.
