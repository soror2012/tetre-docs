General Wing is a decorated warrior and the recently elected leader of Grand Cross. He once led the prestigious special forces group known as the **Destined**, and he created the **Elites** to protect the nation and its people.

He is a responsible, capable leader who wants to keep the peace. He takes personal interest in the Academy's best students, and there is talk that he may revive the title of **Keepers**. He will speak to that himself, "or not at all."

The Professor is one of his close confidants and recommends the four prospects to him.
