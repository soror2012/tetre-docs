# Grand Cross

**Leader:** General Wing · **Military:** The Elites · **Role in the story:** the starting nation

Grand Cross is a hospitable and relatively peaceful nation. Its architecture is cream sandstone with arched stained-glass windows, violet banners and a gold cross-diamond emblem. It has a clock tower, a rail bridge, airships overhead and a famous Academy.

## The government

General Wing was recently elected General. He had previously led a prestigious special forces group called the **Destined**, and he founded the **Elites** to protect the region and its people.

## The Elites

The Elites are an elite military division, and they know it. They are talented, confident to the point of arrogance and convinced they are the best fighting force in Tetre. They believe that if anyone is capable of protecting the world, it is them.

Students have mixed feelings about them:

> "The Elites are brilliant. Everyone says so. They also say so. Constantly. In the cafeteria."

The Elites also guard Grand Cross's portion of the Ward, and they are accepting recruits from the Academy's top prospects.

## Grand Cross Academy

The Academy is where the story begins. Its courtyard has a rose-flagstone plaza, planters, a stag fountain with the Otalla memorial plaque, a willow on the east lawn and a noticeboard. Its top twenty prospects are being watched by the Elites. See [Dialogue & Events](/systems/dialogue) for what the noticeboard says.

## Look

Cream stone, violet and gold heraldry, dark-navy iron lampposts and benches, abundant flowers and ivy.
