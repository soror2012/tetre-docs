# Nations & Factions

Tetre has several nations. These are the established starting powers; others will be added as the world grows.

| Nation / faction | Leader | Force | Known for | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [**Grand Cross**](./grand-cross) | General Wing | The Elites | Hospitality, the Academy, the starting region | Playable in the demo |
| [**Halford**](./halford) | Walter, Master of Arms | The Vultures | Trade and engineering | Mentioned; its soldiers appear |
| [**Narae**](./narae) | Lady Relna, the Paladin | The Templars | Arctic land, advanced magic | Later |
| [**New Otalla**](./new-otalla) | n/a | n/a | A settlement of survivors | Later |
| [**The nature-aligned nation**](./nature-aligned) | n/a | n/a | Coexistence with the natural world | Name to be decided |
| [**The Council**](./council) | n/a | n/a | Court, school and mediator | Center of Tetre |

## What they share

All of them depend on the [Ward](/world/ward). Each keeps or guards its own [Ward Nodes](/world/ward-nodes), and none of them is written as a straightforward villain. A leader can be proud, impatient or wrong without being cruel.

::: info Still being decided
The remaining nations' names, the full geography and how the nations border each other are open questions.
:::
