# Narae

**Leader:** Lady Relna, the Paladin · **Military:** The Templars

Narae is an arctic nation with advanced magical knowledge. Its Templars specialize in defensive magic and balanced combat techniques.

## Lady Relna

Relna is strong-willed, caring and politically assertive. She puts the interests of her people and the survival of Tetre first.

::: info In the game
Narae does not appear in the opening demo.
:::
