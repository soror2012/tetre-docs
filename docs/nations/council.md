# The Council

The Council sits at the geographical center of Tetre. It is part:

- **Supreme court** and international judicial body
- **School** and mentorship organization
- **Neutral diplomat** between nations

It is **not** in charge of how Shards are distributed. Its role is education, mediation, judgment and keeping international law consistent. Students from every nation go there to learn, many of them looking for common ground.

::: info In the game
The Council is not in the opening demo.
:::
