# New Otalla

New Otalla is a settlement of survivors rebuilding after the catastrophe at [Otalla](/world/hollow-zones#otalla). It stands for the human cost of the Hollow danger: people who lost a home and are making a new one.

::: info In the game
New Otalla is planned as a place for character recruitment, side quests and local stories. It is not in the opening demo.
:::
