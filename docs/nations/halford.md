# Halford

**Leader:** Walter, Master of Arms · **Military:** The Vultures

Halford is a thriving trade region, known for engineering expertise and technological development. Its people have long been hospitable and willing to help other nations. Students in Grand Cross follow its news: a tram line is being laid from Halford's engineers to the Academy, and cargo balloons carry Halford tea across the border.

## Walter

Walter is an engineering mastermind and the Master of Arms of Halford. His ambition for his nation is real.

## The Vultures

The Vultures are Halford's tactical soldiers. They use enhanced weaponry and field engineering to get mission outcomes right and efficiently.

They are **disciplined professionals, not monsters.** The art reflects it:

- iron-blue armor with orange visor lights
- angular helmets and engineered gear
- Halford reads as engineering, not menace

In battle they speak matter-of-factly. Enemy types you will meet are on the [Combat page](/systems/combat#enemies): Troopers, Engineers and the heavier Breachers. Their gear is weak to Lightning.
